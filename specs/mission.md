# Mission

SparkBuild helps kids aged 11–16 really learn to code. Sparky, our AI tutor, coaches with hints,
questions and drawings on the board, but it never takes the thinking away. Every line a student
ships is their own.

## Who it is for

- **Students (11–16)** are the users. They learn Python on the board with Sparky.
- **Parents** are the customers. They have their own account linked to their children, see
  progress and invoices, and pay online.
- **Schools** (org admins and teachers) are customers in B2B. They manage their own students and
  classes and pay per student seat.

## Our promise about AI

- **Weeks 1–6: Sparky never writes the answer.** Students build the basics with their own hands.
- **Weeks 7–12: students learn to use AI and still own the thinking.** A separate AI helper may
  write code, but only under four ownership rules:
  1. The student plans first: goal and steps before the AI touches code.
  2. The AI writes small pieces only, a few lines at a time.
  3. The student finds the bug: AI code is not trusted until they have checked it.
  4. The student explains before accepting: code counts only once they can explain it in their own words.

## What success looks like (next 6 months)

- Students finish the 12-week course.
- Families stay and keep paying month after month.

## Scope

- **Now: families (B2C), on a multi-tenant foundation.** Sign-in, student data, a great course,
  parent self-checkout, all built so schools can join without a rebuild.
- **Next: schools (B2B).** Each school gets its own subdomain (`<school>.sparkbuild.space`), adds
  its own teachers, students and classes, and is billed per student seat. We onboard schools
  ourselves first; self-serve signup comes later.

## Non-goals

- No languages other than Python: no HTML, JS or Scratch track.
- No self-serve school signup yet: we create schools ourselves.
- Each student belongs to one organization (B2C or one school).
- No AI-written solutions outside the ownership rules above.
