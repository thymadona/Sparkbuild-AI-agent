A small app with a big heart

Kindness Queue

A tiny tool that helps busy students remember one kind thing they can do for someone today.

The problem

Sometimes people want to help, but they cannot think of an idea in the moment. Small kindnesses get forgotten when life gets busy.

For: busy students

Why try it?

Kindness Queue gives you one doable idea in seconds. No perfect plan needed—just one bright next move.

My first feature

Tap the button to get a tiny act of kindness. Replace this demo with the first useful action for your own app.

Ready for a bright idea?

If I had one more week

I would let people save their favorite ideas and make their own kindness list.

Build notes

My app is for people who want to make a positive difference, one simple action at a time.
